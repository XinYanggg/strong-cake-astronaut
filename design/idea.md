# Strong Cake Astronaut

Strong Cake Astronaut: a 2D top-down lunar outpost survival and resource-management game for kids aged 7 to 12, in landscape for laptop and PC. One player-controlled cake-themed astronaut walks between rooms (Command Center, Greenhouse, Power Station, Radiation Shelter) with WASD, E to interact, mouse for panels, Tab for missions and inventory, M for the map. Survive 5 lunar days (5 real minutes each) by balancing electricity (70 units available against 100 of demand), life support, food and shielding. Real NASA space weather events (with historical replays as fallback) trigger radiation alerts, power shortages and communication delays. Each event has a kid-friendly info panel. Full details are in design/game-design-document.md. Phase 1: no NASA data yet, just the astronaut moving around an outpost of labeled rooms.
